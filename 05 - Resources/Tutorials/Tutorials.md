## 📱 Mobile & Embedded Development

### Flutter & Firebase
1. **Flutter Official Codelabs:** [flutter.dev/codelabs](https://docs.flutter.dev/codelabs) — Hands-on guides for building your first responsive Flutter app.
2. **Firebase for Flutter:** [firebase.google.com/docs/flutter/setup](https://firebase.google.com/docs/flutter/setup) — Initializing Firebase, configuring `google-services.json`, and writing real-time queries.
3. **State Management with Provider:** Practical state management patterns without boilerplate.

### Embedded IoT & ESP32
1. **Random Nerd Tutorials (ESP32):** [randomnerdtutorials.com/projects-esp32](https://randomnerdtutorials.com/projects-esp32/) — Comprehensive guides for ESP32 Wi-Fi, sensors, motor control, and power management.
2. **PlatformIO Getting Started:** Guide to installing PlatformIO via `uv`/VS Code and managing libraries in `platformio.ini`.
