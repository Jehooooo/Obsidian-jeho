# 📅 Weekly Review

> Every Sunday, take 20-30 minutes to reflect on your week. Use the template below to create a new weekly review note.

---

## How to Do a Weekly Review

1. **Create a new note** using the [[Weekly Review Template]]
2. **Name it** `Weekly Review - YYYY-MM-DD` (use the Sunday date)
3. **Save it** in this folder (`00 - Dashboard/`)
4. **Process your [[Inbox]]** — move items to proper folders
5. **Review active projects** — update statuses and tasks
6. **Plan next week** — set priorities
