# 🪞 Reflections

> A space for personal reflections on your journey as a software engineer. Write honestly about what you're learning, struggling with, and feeling.

## How to Use

After a significant experience — finishing a project, solving a hard bug, learning something new, or having a tough day — write a brief reflection here or create a new note.

## Reflections

### 2026-10-19 — Balancing Multiple Stacks & Organizing the Second Brain
- **What happened:** Managing full-stack web applications (Bayanihan Hub, Incident Monitoring), cross-platform mobile & firmware (Automatic Dispensing in Flutter/ESP32), and client consulting (Mayerfeld Practicum). Overhauled the entire Obsidian Second Brain vault to bring clarity to all projects and competencies.
- **How I feel about it:** Energized and confident. Having everything categorized with clear repositories, hardware wiring, and software schemas removes cognitive clutter.
- **What I learned:** Context switching is expensive unless your documentation is crystal clear. Separating daily execution logs from core architecture notes keeps momentum high.
- **What I'll do differently:** Commit to daily note updates at the close of every working session and check off tasks systematically during Sunday weekly reviews.
