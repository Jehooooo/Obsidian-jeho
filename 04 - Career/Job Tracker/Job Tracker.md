# 💼 Job Tracker

> Track your job applications, interviews, and follow-ups here.

## How to Use

1. Add a new row for each application
2. Update status as you progress
3. Review during your [[Weekly Review]]

## Statuses
- **Researching** — looking into the company
- **Applied** — application submitted
- **Phone Screen** — initial call scheduled/completed
- **Interview** — technical/behavioral interview
- **Offer** — received an offer
- **Rejected** — application rejected
- **Withdrawn** — I withdrew
