---

## How to Use

1. **Capture** — Write anything below. Don't worry about formatting.
2. **Tag it** — Add a quick tag: `#idea`, `#bug`, `#learn`, `#project`, `#career`, `#resource`
3. **Move it** — During your weekly review, create proper notes and move items to the right folder.
4. **Clear it** — Delete items from here once they've been processed.
