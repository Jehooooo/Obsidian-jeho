# 📅 Daily Notes & Engineering Log

> Daily capture for engineering tasks, bugs, solutions, learnings, and reflections.

---

## 🎯 How to Use Daily Notes

1. Press `Ctrl + P` in Obsidian and select **"Daily notes: Open today's note"** (or click the calendar ribbon icon).
2. Notes are automatically generated from the [[Daily Note Template]] and saved in this folder (`01 - Daily Notes/`).
3. Use your daily note throughout the day to log:
   - What you are actively working on
   - Problems and blockers encountered
   - Specific code solutions and command snippets
   - Quick learnings and reflection
